Front desk dashboardThe live picture of the day — awaiting approval, arrivals, departures and free rooms — with the full booking list, quick filter tabs, date and search filters, one-click row actions, and a sound alert on every screen when a new booking arrives.2. Taking a booking at the front deskPick the dates, see every sellable rate grouped by room type with live prices, choose the room by floor, find or create the guest with their identity document, and confirm. The booking recordOne screen per booking from creation to departure: rooms, rates, guest, totals, payment status, and approve / decline / cancel / check-in / check-out. Guest booking on your websiteThe same availability engine on the public site — search bar, results by room type, room pages with photos and amenities, and a booking form matching the front desk. Manual payment, invoice and confirmation   NEWPayment instructions you write yourself, shown on screen and e-mailed with a numbered PDF invoice. A payment deadline per booking, staff recording of amount / method / reference, partial payments, automatic receipts, a full payment history that is never overwritten, and a follow-up list of overdue bookings.6. Rooms, floors and inventoryRoom types, shared named floors, and every physical room with its number, floor and state (available, maintenance, out of service). Rate plans and pricingYour stay windows as a reusable library — Half Day, Extended Half Day, Rest Time, Overnight, Extended Overnight, 24 Hours Fixed and Flexible — each priced separately per room type, with sale prices, minimum stay, seasonal pricing, occupancy pricing, and early-bird and last-minute discounts.8. Availability calendar and booking rulesA date-by-rate calendar with per-date price overrides, per-date open/closed switches and bulk updates. Guest recordsEvery guest with their contact details, identity document on file, full stay history, notes with author and timestamp, and the ability to ban a guest. Returning guests are recognised by name, phone or e-mail.10. ReportsYour Sales, Rooms and Room Availability reports rebuilt as they are — with the arrival-date versus creation-date switch — plus new occupancy percentage, revenue by room type and rate plan, and revenue by staff member. Exporting and archiving dataBooking export to Excel or CSV for any date range, with the optional archive-and-remove you have today, a library of past files kept in a protected folder, plus guest-list export and scheduled automatic exports.12.
